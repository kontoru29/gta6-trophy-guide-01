The road to Vice Got a Price!

Around 200 hours, difficulty about 4 out of 10, all single-player. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals: the list is long, not hard. Play the story first, then clean up the map — that order keeps you from redoing activities and gets the chapter trophies out of the way early.

Do this first: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. The game has no mission replay, so a manual save is your only way back. And leave cheats off: using a cheat disables trophies and saving.

  1. Phase 1 — The story: Chapters 1 to the final mission 50–80 hours

    Play the campaign straight through. The chapter trophies (Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby!, Top Of The World, Ma) and the mission trophies (Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory) all pop as you play, and the final mission earns the gold You Gotta Know When to Get Off. Jason and Lucia are both playable from Chapter 2, which opens the partner trophies.

    While you are here: do Lucia's balcony meditation before leaving Chapter 1 — it counts toward Positive Mental Attitude and is the one step that can disappear. Scan vehicles and study animals as you travel; it all counts later.

  2. Phase 2 — Strangers and side characters 10–20 hours

    Finish the stranger mission strands: the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, Bret's unique animal hunt, and the 8 strands that earn Strangers & Freaks (silver). These unlock as markers on the map, so sweep them as they appear rather than leaving them all for the end.

  3. Phase 3 — Activities 20–30 hours

    Work the activities: gold medals in all 42 Shooting Range challenges (The Shootist), a Top Fun drone race win at all 14 locations, 20 different race wins, beating Cal at fishing, one arcade game, a bowling game, and all 18 meditation spots. Try each of the 9 activity types once for Master of None, and push the challenge count toward 50.

  4. Phase 4 — Collectibles and exploration 20–30 hours

    Finish the map work: 3 collectible card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, 100 vehicles discovered, all 52 areas of Leonida explored, all 8 garage properties bought, and 10 vehicles registered to the Scooter Bros app. Sweep one region at a time and use the in-game collectible trackers.

  5. Phase 5 — Crime and money 15–25 hours

    Do the crime set: 5 bank vaults robbed and escaped, an armored truck, a jewelry store robbed without being detected, one illegal operation for each of the 9 gangs, 250 stunts, a 4-star wanted level lost with your partner, 3 shops robbed back-to-back with your partner, and the money trophies — $250,000 laundered in Marked Bills and $100,000 spent across all shops.

  6. Phase 6 — Clean-up and 100% the rest

    Open the 35-task 100% checklist in the Progress menu and work it top to bottom. Progress made earlier counts and nothing on it can be missed. When A Long Road (gold) pops, Vice Got a Price! follows immediately — that is the platinum.

Roadmap order and time estimates follow the platinum roadmaps published by KeenGamer and Of Zen and Computing, and the trophy lists published by GTA Zone (PowerPyx and GTABase) and Output Lag. Full links on the Sources tab.

Missable trophies

Good news first: the guides agree that only one trophy is potentially missable. Everything else unlocks whenever you get around to it, in free roam after the story if you like.

How to stay safe anyway

  • Manual saves. Keep a save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so a save is the only way back to a chapter.
  • Do the Chapter 1 meditation early. Lucia's balcony meditation counts toward Positive Mental Attitude and disappears after Chapter 1. Do it before the chapter ends.
  • Leave cheats off. Using a cheat disables trophies and saving, according to the trophy guides.
  • Nothing on the 100% checklist is missable. The 35-task checklist in the Progress menu can all be finished after the story.

Missable status: PowerPyx via GTA Zone, KeenGamer, Output Lag and GTA Vibes all flag Positive Mental Attitude as the only potentially missable trophy. Community discussion notes the rest may not be missable at all; treat that as unconfirmed until more players finish a run.
